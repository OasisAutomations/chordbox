# Chordbox

A pocket chord synthesizer that runs in the browser. Seven diatonic chord keys, an XY morph pad, sound presets, arpeggiator, and a simple bar loop recorder. Installable PWA — works offline after the first load. Audio stays on your device.

**Live:** https://oasisautomations.github.io/chordbox/

## How to play

1. Open the link on your phone (portrait) and tap **Tap to play** to unlock audio.
2. Hold one or more of the seven cream keys — each is a diatonic chord in the current key (I–vii). Multitouch works.
3. Hold and drag the round pad: **up** adds a 7th, **down** makes it sus4, **left** flips major/minor, **right** adds a 9th. The pad also closes the filter toward the edges.
4. Small buttons: **KEY** (root + major/minor), **OCT**, **SND** (Pad / Keys / Pluck / Organ), **ARP**, **BPM**, **FX** (Dry / Room / Hall / Echo).
5. Loop bar: metronome ♩, ● record/overdub a 1-bar loop, ▶ play, CLR clear.
